Privacy Policy

How WF Pundit collects, uses and looks after information when you visit the site or use a reader account.

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This page explains the information WF Pundit handles when you use the website. It is a practical notice, not a legal opinion. We recommend obtaining qualified legal review before relying on it as your final compliance document.

1. Who operates WF Pundit

WF Pundit operates wfpundit.uk, a women's-football news and reader-community website. For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@wfpundit.uk.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your display name and email address. For email/password accounts, we store a password hash rather than your plain-text password.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose Google sign-in, we receive account information Google provides, including your Google account identifier, email address and, where available, display name. We use this to authenticate and maintain your WF Pundit account.
  • Reader activity: articles you save, comments and replies you submit, reactions you choose, and reading or notification preferences you set.
  • Newsletter choices: if you separately subscribe, your email address and subscription details are processed by our newsletter provider. Creating a reader account does not subscribe you.
  • Technical information: our hosting and security services may process connection, device, browser and request information in their ordinary operation, including information needed to protect and operate the website.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide sign-in and password recovery, display saved articles, publish and moderate reader comments, apply your preferences, operate notifications you have enabled, administer newsletter subscriptions, maintain website security, and respond to support or privacy requests.

4. Cookies and sessions

When you sign in, WF Pundit uses a first-party session cookie to keep you authenticated. The session cookie is configured as HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. It is necessary for account features. Your browser may also store your selected display theme locally. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although blocking necessary cookies may prevent account features from working.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate the website and its features. These currently include Cloudflare for hosting and database services, Google for optional Google sign-in, Resend for account-related email such as welcome and password-reset messages, Brevo for separately requested newsletter delivery, and GitHub for website code and certain image-upload workflows. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy documentation. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, users or our rights.

6. Newsletter choices

Newsletter subscription is optional and separate from account registration. You must actively request subscription. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter or through available account settings. Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not close your WF Pundit account or disable essential account messages such as password resets.

7. Retention and account requests

We keep account and reader information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods may depend on the type of record and operational requirements. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information by emailing admin@wfpundit.uk. We may need to verify your request. Some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it, or where necessary to protect the service and other users.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the data-protection laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; to ask us to restrict or stop certain processing; or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection authority. Contact us at the address above to make a privacy request. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect account information, including password hashing and secure session-cookie settings. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a unique password and keep your account credentials private.

10. Children and eligibility

WF Pundit is a general-audience football news and community website. If you are not legally able to agree to the Terms of Service where you live, please do not create an account without the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date and version shown above. Where a change materially affects how account information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to users' attention.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: admin@wfpundit.uk.
